About Our Institution

Fostering Academic Distinction & Moral Integrity

The Roman Catholic Diocese of Palayamkottai has been rendering exemplary service in the field of higher education since 1973 under the Chairmanship of the Bishop of Palayamkottai. St. Joseph College of Arts and Science was founded by Rt. Rev. A. Jude Paulraj on 13.06.2016, blessed and inaugurated by Rev. Dr. A. Antony Pappusamy, and established in 2018.

Under the inspirational motto “To know oneself and To know God”, the college is growing with a noble vision to uplift the rural and marginalized youth in and around Tenkasi and Tirunelveli districts through holistic, affordable, and world-class collegiate education.
