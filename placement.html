Step-by-Step Flow

The Campus Placement Process

A transparent and student-centric placement cycle designed to connect candidates with matching career tracks.

1

Registration & Profiling

Final year students submit their academic transcripts, verified CGPA, and career interests through the TAP portal.

2

Intensive Prep Bootcamp

Mandatory 2-week pre-drive brush-up covering quantitative reasoning, domain technical viva, and resume refinement.

3

Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)

Corporate delegates present company culture, job descriptions, compensation breakdown, and growth avenues.

4

Assessment & Interviews

Computerized aptitude tests followed by group discussions, technical face-to-face interviews, and HR rounds.

5

Offer & Onboarding

Distribution of formal offer letters followed by guidance for document verification and corporate joining.
