Academics & Departments

Offering robust 3-Year Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) Undergraduate programmes affiliated to Manonmaniam Sundaranar University, Tirunelveli.

Faculties of Study

Academic Departments

Each department is led by qualified educators committed to mentoring students from foundational concepts to competitive professional capability.

Faculty of Science

Department of Computer Science

Degree Awarded: B.Sc. Computer Science

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with Mathematics / Computer Science

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

The B.Sc. Computer Science programme develops solid foundations in computational theory, C++, Python, Java programming, Data Structures, Relational Database Management Systems (RDBMS), Operating Systems, Web Technologies, and Cloud Computing basics. Practical sessions are held in our air-conditioned computer center.

Python & Data Structures Web Development DBMS & Software Engineering IT Placement Assistance
Apply for B.Sc. CS
Faculty of Science

Department of Mathematics

Degree Awarded: B.Sc. Mathematics

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with Mathematics

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

Offers rigorous training in classical and modern mathematics including Classical Algebra, Real & Complex Analysis, Differential Equations, Vector Calculus, Operations Research, Graph Theory, and Statistics. Prepares graduates for careers in data analytics, actuarial sciences, competitive civil services (TNPSC/UPSC), and higher education (M.Sc., B.Ed., Ph.D.).

Real & Complex Analysis Differential Equations Operations Research TNPSC / Bank Coaching

Faculty of Science

Department of Physics

Degree Awarded: B.Sc. Physics

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with Physics & Mathematics

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

Covers Mechanics, Thermal Physics, Optics & Spectroscopy, Electromagnetism, Quantum Mechanics, Nuclear Physics, Solid State Physics, and Digital Electronics. Students spend substantial laboratory hours applying theoretical principles into empirical practice using precision apparatus.

Precision Physics Lab Digital Electronics Optics & Acoustics Scientific Research Pathways

Faculty of Commerce

Department of Commerce

Degree Awarded: B.Com. (General)

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with Commerce / Accountancy

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

Delivers foundational competency in Financial Accounting, Cost & Management Accounting, Corporate Law, Business Economics, Income Tax Law and Practice, Auditing, and Computerized Accounting (Tally ERP). Tailored for aspirants aiming for CA, CMA, CS, Banking, and Corporate Financial roles.

Financial & Cost Accounting Income Tax & GST Tally & Computerized Acc CA/CMA Foundation Support
Apply for B.Com
Faculty of Commerce

Dept. of Corporate Secretaryship

Degree Awarded: B.Com. Corporate Secretaryship

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with Commerce / Arts / Science

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

A specialized business law and managerial secretarial degree. Topics include Company Secretarial Practice, Industrial Law, Corporate Governance, Institutional Drafting, Commercial Correspondence, Securities Law, and Business Ethics. Directly maps into careers in corporate secretarial departments, compliance consulting, and CS (ICSI) qualifications.

Company Law & Secretarial Practice Securities Law & Governance Industrial & Labor Laws Corporate Compliance

Faculty of Humanities

Department of English

Degree Awarded: B.A. English Literature

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with English (Any stream)

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

Extensive immersion into British Literature, American Literature, Indian Writing in English, Shakespearean Drama, Literary Criticism, English Phonetics, and Spoken English Communication. Equips students with fluent communicative mastery, creative expression, and critical thinking essential for teaching, content creation, civil services, and corporate communications.

British & World Literatures Phonetics & Oral Fluency Critical & Cultural Theory Media & Content Writing

Department of Tamil (தமிழ்த்துறை)

Degree Awarded: B.A. Tamil

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Affiliation: MSU Tirunelveli
Eligibility: +2 with Tamil (Any stream)

Program Highlights & Curriculum Focus

Scholarly exploration of Sangam literature, Silappathikaram, Manimekalai, Bakthi literature, modern poetry, dramatic forms, Tolkappiyam grammar, Tamil journalism, creative writing, and regional folklore. Hands-on Science

Laboratories & Practical Training

Bridging the gap between conceptual theory and real-world empirical experimentation.

Computer Science Lab

Equipped with high-performance desktop terminals running Linux and Windows, Python compilers, Oracle/MySQL databases, and high-speed broadband connectivity for programming, web development, and digital literacy training.

General Physics Lab

Houses precision spectrometers, traveling microscopes, cathode ray oscilloscopes (CRO), thermal conductivity benches, and electronic breadboards giving physics scholars experimental clarity.

Ventilated Lecture Halls

Two-story academic wings featuring spacious, sun-lit classrooms equipped with modern presentation aids, acoustic podiums, and comfortable seating overlooking the peaceful courtyard gardens.

Administrative Foyer & Office

Airy entrance breezeway housing the Student Affairs Counter, Principal’s Suite, examination coordination desk, and notice boards for daily announcements and institutional services.
