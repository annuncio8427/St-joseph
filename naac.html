Quality Assurance & Institutional Excellence

NAAC & IQAC Accreditation Portal

St. Joseph College of Arts and Science is committed to the highest standards of higher education quality, continuous evaluation, and compliance with the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) 7-Criteria framework.

Internal Quality Assurance

Institutional Quality Culture

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at SJCAS was constituted to develop a conscious, consistent, and catalytic quality improvement mechanism across curricular delivery, teaching-learning, faculty research, student progression, and institutional governance.

Regular Academic & Administrative Audits (AAA)
Student Feedback & Satisfaction Survey (SSS)
Green, Energy & Environment Campus Audits
Faculty Development Programmes (FDP) & Seminars
ICT-Enabled Teaching & Smart Classrooms

7-Criteria Model

NAAC Seven Assessment Criteria

Comprehensive institutional framework evaluating educational quality, student support, and governance.

I

Curricular Aspects

Curriculum design and implementation in alignment with Manonmaniam Sundaranar University CBCS patterns, academic flexibility, feedback systems, and value-added courses.

Curricular Policy & Implementation Value Added & Skill Courses
II

Teaching-Learning & Evaluation

Student enrollment diversity, student-teacher ratio, experiential ICT learning, continuous internal assessment (CIA) reforms, and learning outcomes attainment.

Student Satisfaction Survey (SSS) CIA Evaluation Framework
III

Research, Innovation & Extension

Faculty research publications, seminar grants, EDI entrepreneurial incubation, and robust community outreach through NSS, YRC, and Enviro clubs.

Faculty Research Publications NSS Outreach & Extension Impact
IV

Infrastructure & Learning Resources

Adequacy of high-tech computing labs, physics experimental darkrooms, central digital library with N-LIST e-resources, sports complex, and Wi-Fi connectivity.

Library & IT Resources Audit Sports & Physical Infrastructure
V

Student Support & Progression

Diocesan and government scholarships, career counseling and competitive exam coaching, placement assistance, alumni association, and zero-tolerance anti-ragging cell.

Scholarship Beneficiary Details Placement & Higher Studies Records
VI

Governance, Leadership & Management

Participative management under the Roman Catholic Diocese of Palayamkottai, 17 statutory committees, financial management, e-governance in office, and welfare measures.

Institutional Strategic Plan Staff Welfare & Financial Audits
VII

Institutional Values & Best Practices

Eco-friendly green campus, rainwater harvesting, solar energy initiatives, inclusive gender equity, code of conduct, and distinctiveness in rural youth empowerment.

Green & Environmental Audit Institutional Best Practices
