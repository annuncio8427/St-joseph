Safe Student Mobility

College Bus & Transit Routes

Providing safe, comfortable, and reliable daily transportation for students and staff spanning the major towns and rural areas of Tenkasi District.

Safe & Supervised

Experienced drivers, speed limiters, and female faculty escorts on every route.

Punctual Timings

Synchronized with morning assembly (8:45 AM) and evening departure (4:15 PM).

Subsidized Pass

Nominal term-wise transport fees designed to accommodate rural families.

Wide Coverage

Serving 25+ boarding points across Kadayam, Tenkasi, Pavoorchatram & Alangulam.

Route Schedules

Designated College Bus Routes

Morning pickup schedules and key designated boarding points.

Route 1

Tenkasi – Vaikalipatti (Direct)

Departs Tenkasi: 7:45 AM

Major Boarding Stops: Tenkasi Old Bus Stand → New Bus Stand → Melagaram → Ayikudi → Sambavar Vadakarai → Kadayam → Mettur → SJCAS Campus (8:35 AM).

Route 2

Alangulam – Nettur – Vaikalipatti

Departs Alangulam: 7:50 AM

Major Boarding Stops: Alangulam Bus Stand → Nettur → Sivalarkulam → Ravanasamudram → Kadayam Railway Gate → SJCAS Campus (8:35 AM).

Route 3

Pavoorchatram – Kizhapuliyur – Vaikalipatti

Departs Pavoorchatram: 7:55 AM

Major Boarding Stops: Pavoorchatram Market → Keelapavoor → Avudayanoor → Kallurani → Mettur → SJCAS Campus (8:35 AM).

Route 4

Surandai – Veerakeralampudur – Vaikalipatti

Departs Surandai: 7:40 AM

Major Boarding Stops: Surandai Bus Stand → Veerakeralampudur → Uthumalai → Kadayam → Mettur → SJCAS Campus (8:35 AM).

Need Bus Pass or Route Assistance?

Students can apply for the subsidized bus pass during college admission or contact the transport in-charge at the campus office.
