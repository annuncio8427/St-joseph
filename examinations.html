System of Assessment

MSU Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

St. Joseph College of Arts and Science adheres strictly to the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) framework prescribed by Manonmaniam Sundaranar University (MSU), Tirunelveli.

The academic evaluation consists of two complementary components: Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) carrying 25% weightage and the University End-Semester Examination carrying 75% weightage.

Internal Assessment (25 Marks)
University Exam (75 Marks)
Mandatory 75% Attendance
Two Mid-Semester Internal Tests
