About Us
About As
The Roman Catholic diocese of Palayamkottai is rendering service in the field of Education since 1973 Under the Chairmanship of the Bishop of Palayamkottai. St.	Joseph College of Arts and Science was founded by Rt. Rev. A. Jude Paulraj on 13.06.2016 and blessed and inaugurated by Rev. Dr. A. Antony Pappusamuy the Apostolic Administrator of  Palayamkottai Diocese. St Joseph College of Arts and Science established in 2018 with the motto of “To know oneself and To know God” is managed by the R. C. Diocese of Palayamkottai. It is a self-financed Coeducational College of Arts and Science at Vaikalipatti in Tenkasi District affiliated to the Manonmaniam Sundaranar University, Tirunelveli.  It is offering 6 UG Courses. We are proud of our college today it is growing with a noble vision to serve and uplift the poor and the downtrodden in and around the area by giving them quality education.

Principal Greeting Message
Dear Parents, students and well-wishers of the St Joseph's family, my prayerful Greetings to you all. First and foremost I would like to express my sincere gratitude to every one of you for your constant support, love and prayerful concern towards our College which enables and encourages us to strive hard to carry forward the mission of spreading value - based knowledge, wisdom and education to one and all.
Education is a multistage process of building character, acquiring knowledge and improving skills for life - long learning to lead a happy, healthy and peaceful life. Our Joseph College not only focuses on the theoretical curriculum but also helps in the development of the student personality,  extra - curricular activities and over all perspective. The objective of higher education in rural area is associated with employment as it ensures a positive attitude to see the universe from a better angle and to analyze, determine and apply an individual idea in a constructive development of the society.
The inspiration and commitment of our management to continuous improvement and the hard work of the faculty members towards the overall growth of our students ensures that every student passing out of our College will taste the success in any sphere of life they choose to work. I hope that their desire to contribute for the benefit of the society grows with their happiness and prosperity in life.

Secretary Message
Echoing the motto “To know oneself and to know God”, St Joseph college of Arts And Science, situated in the rural area of Vaikalipatti, is an admirable self-finance coeducation college run by the R.C. Diocese of Palayamkottai.  Witnessing to the human values and respect, the institution is always keen on forming the integral personality of the students.  God fearing people will be treasured with wisdom not just with the knowledge.  The knowledge received from studies and various programmes is transmuted to the higher gift of wisdom through which the youth of our college understand the importance of principles of life, the way to face the challenges they come across on the way and find real joy of life.  God given gifts are to be utilized for the growth of the society and the progress of each one in families.  The institution takes steps to find out the hidden talents of the students and gives opportunity to grow in them through various cocurricular activities conducted in both inside and outside the college.  Though the institution experiences the challenges, ups and downs at its daily activities, we, the staff and students, learn even from the obstacles and riddles and thus have courage to do better and to be the best.  We learn to walk together in hope.  The journey includes the parents, Alumni and the well-wishers of our college. The way we have to go is too long but we join hands with one another. The people around us are proud to look at our college as a sign of their prestige and blessing to the generations.  The management is so delighted to serve the people of these areas and to participate in the future of their life. We rely on God’s power who blesses the humanity in Peace and Joy.
