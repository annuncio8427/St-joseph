Transit & Route Guidance

  • From Tenkasi: Regular buses operate toward Kadayam / Mettur (approx. 18 km).
  • From Tirunelveli: Buses ply via Alangulam and Kadayam toward Mettur / Vaikalipatti.
  • Nearest Railway Stations: Kadayam (KKY), Pavoorchatram (PCM), Tenkasi Jn (TSI).
  • College Bus Facility: Dedicated daily transport covers Kadayam, Tenkasi, Pavoorchatram, Alangulam, Surandai, and surrounding villages.
