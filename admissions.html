Verification

Documents Required at Admission

  • Original +2 / Higher Secondary Mark Statement (with 3 photocopies).
  • 10th Standard / SSLC Mark Sheet photocopy (for DOB proof).
  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) from school last studied.
  • Conduct Certificate from the Head of the Institution.
  • Permanent Community Certificate (ST / SC / SCA / MBC / DNC / BC).
  • Aadhaar Card photocopy of student and parent.
  • 5 recent passport-size colour photographs.
Financial Aid

Scholarships & Fee Concessions

In line with the mission of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Palayamkottai to uplift rural and economically disadvantaged families, multiple scholarship avenues are facilitated:

  • Post-Matric Govt Scholarships: Full support for SC/ST and converted Christian scholars.
  • BC/MBC Welfare Grants: State government fee assistance schemes.
  • Diocesan Concessions: Special discretionary fee subsidies for deserving poor students.
  • First Graduate Concession: Guidance for first-generation collegiate entrants.
